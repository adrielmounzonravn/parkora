# Parkora

**Reserve an on-street parking spot before you leave home.**
Parkora lets drivers reserve a spot on city-managed streets for a date and time window, so they arrive knowing they have somewhere to park. The city sets rates, zones, and fines; Parkora takes a commission per reservation.

*Name:* park + *hora* (Spanish for "hour"): your reserved hour.

## In one minute

- **Who:** drivers who go at least weekly to a hard-to-park area (downtown, markets, hospitals, universities) and stay 1 to 4 hours. The paying customer is the **city government** (B2G).
- **Experiment:** survey of drivers in Cochabamba, Bolivia, with pass/fail criteria set before the responses. 7 responses: 2 of 3 criteria passed (they can plan ahead, they'd pay the same or more), 1 failed (minutes lost looking for parking), and 0 of 7 left contact info.
- **Biggest open assumption:** that a city government wants to run it. Next test: meeting with Cochabamba's city government, plus a pre-registration landing page.
